External attenuation

The total between the PicoFox and the radio (cabled) or its antenna: the attenuators as set now, plus fixed pads and cables.

Turn on cabled mode?

Cabled mode sends no Morse ID, and lets RF turn on without a callsign.

Use it only when the PicoFox output goes into a load or test equipment through cables and attenuators, never to an antenna.

You'll be asked once per session of the app. The PicoFox forgets cabled mode when it powers off.
