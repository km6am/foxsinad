Setup

Once per session: the session (summit) name, your callsign and the frequency; then the radio under test. Cabled: the PicoFox goes through attenuators into the radio's antenna jack. Over the air: it radiates, through an attenuator, while the radio is on its own antenna.

Audio

Start transmits the tone at full level (strongest signal). Open the radio's squelch and turn its volume until the audio bar sits in the target band; it's recorded after 2 s on target and goes with each run.

Level

Start transmits at the ballpark attenuation. Change the external attenuator until SINAD reads about 12 dB (the green band), Stop, then enter the total external attenuation (attenuators and cables). Cabled, that makes levels the level at the radio; over the air, it's a note of the setup.

Sweep

Steps the PicoFox down through its range and finds the 12 dB SINAD point. Repeat runs it several times; their spread is the honest error bar.

Turn on cabled mode?

Cabled mode sends no Morse ID, and lets RF turn on without a callsign.

Use it only when the PicoFox output goes into a load or test equipment through cables and attenuators, never to an antenna.

You'll be asked once per session of the app. The PicoFox forgets cabled mode when it powers off.
